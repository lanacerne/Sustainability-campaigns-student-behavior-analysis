# Student Sustainability Survey Analysis | Python

## Overview

This project presents a quantitative research study analyzing the impact of sustainability campaigns on student awareness, participation, and behavioral change. The research focuses on the case of the **Sustainability Week at the School of Economics and Business, University of Ljubljana**.

The study was presented at the **ŠTeKam 2025 Student Technical Conference**.

## Research Objective

The aim of the research was to examine:
- How sustainability campaigns influence student behavior and awareness
- Which communication channels are most effective in encouraging participation
- Whether personal motivation has a stronger impact on sustainable behavior than campaign participation
- Which campaign activities contribute most to behavioral change

## Dataset

- Survey respondents: **516 undergraduate students**
- Data collection method: Online questionnaire (1KA)
- Research approach: Quantitative analysis

## Methodology

The analysis included:
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Exploratory data analysis
- Statistical analysis
- Regression modeling
- Data visualization

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- Statsmodels
- Seaborn
- Statistical analysis
- Data visualization

## Statistical Analysis

The research included:
- Linear regression models to examine relationships between campaign participation, awareness, and behavioral change
- Logistic regression models to identify factors influencing participation and perceived campaign effectiveness

## Key Findings

- 65% of students were aware of the Sustainability Week campaign, but 69% did not participate in any activity.
- Personal motivation was an important predictor of sustainable behavior.
- Interactive activities had a stronger impact on perceived behavioral change.
- Physical communication channels, such as posters and faculty webpages, were more effective predictors of participation than some digital channels.
- 86% of students expressed interest in having more sustainability campaigns.

## Research Output

The final research paper includes:
- Theoretical background on sustainability behavior
- Survey methodology
- Statistical analysis
- Visualization of results
- Practical recommendations for improving sustainability campaigns
